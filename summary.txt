World news, Wednesday 11 November 2026.

US-brokered talks between Russia and Ukraine entered a second day in Abu Dhabi after nearly four hours Tuesday, the first such meeting in nine months. Volodymyr Zelenskyy called it constructive (BBC, PBS NewsHour).

Donald Trump said the Iran war was "effectively over" after meeting Qatar's premier, amid the US blockade, with oil at two-month lows. Secretary Rubio said there was no need to strike Iran again (Arab News, Reuters). An ADNOC vessel was damaged and three tankers caught fire in the Strait of Hormuz (UKMTO).

US prosecutors charged three men over an alleged Iran-backed plot against RAF Fairford in Britain (Justice Department).

Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent urged more balanced US-China trade — "de-risk, not decouple" — before a China trip next week; he has moved onto a military base after Iranian threats (South China Morning Post, The New York Times).

Anthropic filed to list on Nasdaq as ANT, seeking $64 billion, with Nvidia investing $10 billion (The New York Times). TSMC reported insatiable chip demand and a third-quarter dividend, while planning stakes in Anthropic and OpenAI (Nikkei Asia).

US consumer prices rose 0.4% in October, above forecasts, raising Fed rate-rise expectations (US Labor Department).

At COP31 in Antalya, a UN report found only 64 of 136 countries had early-warning capacity on all four measures (Reuters).

Israel's new Knesset was sworn in; opposition leader Gadi Eisenkot vowed to form a government (BBC). Cristiano Ronaldo, 41, retired from international football with record 234 caps and 146 goals for Portugal. Indonesia's Vice-President Gibran told Prabowo he intends to resign after the Supreme Court voided his school-equivalency certificate. The US House voted 211-204 to send Hegseth's impeachment to committee, effectively killing it; Rep. Thomas Massie was the sole Republican dissenter (NPR). A bus in Seongnam hit 32 vehicles and injured 57 people, nine seriously (Yonhap).
